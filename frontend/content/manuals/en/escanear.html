Module · Scan

Read a QR

Scanning the QR of a box or a pallet opens its record instantly: what it contains, what status it is in and its history.

Panel › Scan · /dashboard/scan

What it does. Looks up a box or pallet by its QR code and shows its record.

How it works. It is a lookup by code: you point at the QR, you do not browse a list. Codes are not enumerable by design.

What it shows when you scan

Depending on the QR you read, the record adapts to what the physical item is.

Box

Product, quantity, batch, expiry, status and when it was sealed, plus its change history.

Pallet

The boxes it groups and their status.

How to use it

1

Open Scan

Go to the Scan module from the panel.

2

Point the camera at the code

Focus on the QR code printed on the box or pallet label.

3

The record opens

Use it to confirm what the physical box or pallet in front of you is.

The record is public too

The same minimal record is public at /qr/{code}: anyone with the QR can see it without logging in.

It only shows inventory information, never personal data.

Permissions

Any operator (volunteer, coordinator, national_admin) can scan within the panel. The public QR record does not require a login.
