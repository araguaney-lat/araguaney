Module · Transfers

Move inventory between centers

A transfer moves sealed boxes from one center to another, with approval in between and end-to-end traceability.

Panel › Transfers · /dashboard/transfers

At a glance

What it does. Lets you request, approve and receive box transfers between centers.

Why it matters. It lets you rebalance stock toward where it is needed without losing track of any box.

The state of a transfer

Every status change is recorded with who made it and when.

Normal path of a transfer, from when it is requested until the destination receives it.

REQUESTED → APPROVED → IN_TRANSIT → RECEIVED

or REJECTED from REQUESTED

Step by step

1

The origin creates the transfer

The origin center chooses the destination center and selects the boxes it wants to move. It stays in REQUESTED.

A box cannot be transferred if it is on a pallet or in another active transfer.
2

It is approved (or rejected)

At the origin center the request is reviewed and either approved (moves to APPROVED) or rejected (moves to REJECTED).

3

The origin marks it in transit

Once approved, the origin marks the transfer in transit and it moves to IN_TRANSIT: the boxes are now on their way.

4

The destination receives it

The destination center confirms receipt and the transfer moves to RECEIVED. The boxes now belong to the destination.

Which box can be transferred

A box must meet all of these conditions to be included.

Sealed
It must be in SEALED status. Draft boxes cannot be moved.
From the origin center
The box must belong to the center that creates the transfer.
Not on a pallet
If the box is assigned to a pallet, it cannot be transferred.
Not in another active transfer
The box cannot already be included in another transfer in progress.
Origin ≠ destination
The origin center and the destination center must be different.

Who does each step

Origin center

Creates the transfer, approves or rejects it, and marks it in transit.

Destination center

Receives the transfer and confirms that the boxes arrived.

The national_admin can step in at any of the steps, in any center.

When something fails

These are the messages you will see when a transfer cannot be completed.

✕

Same center

You chose the same center as origin and as destination.

Origin and destination must differ
✕

The box is not sealed

You tried to transfer a box that is not yet in SEALED status.

Box … must be SEALED to transfer
✕

The box is on a pallet

The box is already part of a pallet and cannot be moved separately.

Box … is assigned to a pallet
✕

The box is already in another transfer

The box is included in another active transfer.

Box … is already in an active transfer
✕

Only the destination receives

Only the coordinator of the destination center can confirm receipt.

Only the destination coordinator can receive

Permissions

The coordinator can only create transfers where their own center is involved. Approving, rejecting and sending is done by the coordinator of the origin center; receiving is done by the coordinator of the destination center.

The national_admin can perform any of these actions in any center.
