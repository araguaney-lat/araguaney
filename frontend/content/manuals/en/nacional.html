Module · National Dashboard

The view of all centers

The national dashboard sums the stock of every connected center and answers, at a glance, what there is, how much and where.

Panel › National · /dashboard/national

What it does. Aggregates the inventory of the whole coordination: it brings into a single view what each center has recorded.

Why it exists. It is what sets Araguaney apart. It is not just another inventory, it is the common standard plus the aggregation.

What it aggregates

The dashboard summarizes the national inventory from four complementary angles.

By category

How much there is of each type: medicines, food, water and the other categories.

By center

How much each connected center has, to compare and balance among them.

By medicine (INN)

The active ingredients available nationally, identified by their INN.

Totals and weight

Overall summary and aggregated weight by campaign or by center.

Why only the national_admin sees it

The national_admin has no center assigned: their center_id is null. Since their view is not filtered to a center, they see everything.

The other roles do have a center assigned, so they see only their own center and not the national aggregate.

From inventory to coordination

An isolated inventory helps one center. Aggregation helps an entire region.

Because every center uses the same standard, their stock adds up with a single query and makes decisions possible at the national level.

Permissions

The national dashboard is exclusive to the national_admin. The volunteer and coordinator see the stock of their own center, not the aggregate.
