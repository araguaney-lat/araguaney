Manual · How Araguaney works

From collection to shipment, in order

Araguaney gives every collection center the same standard: record donations item by item, pack them into homogeneous boxes with QR codes, consolidate them into pallets and shipments with a manifest, and see the stock of every center in a national dashboard.

For volunteers, coordinators and national_admins Operates in /dashboard No personal donor data
The flow

Five steps, one piece at a time

Every donation follows the same path. Nothing skips steps: a box does not enter a pallet until it is sealed, and a pallet does not enter a shipment until it is closed.

1 · Intake

The donation is recorded item by item: product, quantity, batch, expiry.

2 · Box

One product, one batch, one expiry. Sealed with a QR code and label.

3 · Pallet

Groups sealed boxes. Can be mixed. It also has its own QR code.

4 · Shipment

Groups closed pallets. Once dispatched, everything is frozen.

5 · Manifest

Exportable packing list, box by box, ready for customs.
Vocabulary

The six core concepts

Everything in Araguaney revolves around these pieces. If you understand these six, you understand the app.

Center tenant

Each collection center is an isolated space. You see only your inventory; the national dashboard adds up everyone's.

ProductType the SKU

The type of product, distinguished by its attributes. Ibuprofen 500 mg and 900 mg are different products.

Intake intake

The record of an incoming donation. It carries an optional free-text donor field, with no personal data.

Box box

Homogeneous: one product, one batch, one expiry. It has a QR code and a printable label.

Pallet pallet

Groups sealed boxes for transport. It can carry different products. With its own QR code.

Shipment shipment

Groups pallets and generates the manifest. Once marked as shipped, it freezes its contents.

Statuses

Each piece has its own state machine

Statuses move in a single direction, and every change is recorded (who and when). A piece does not advance if the previous one is not ready.

Box

Born as a draft when recorded. Only a sealed box can enter a pallet.

DRAFT→ SEALED→ SHIPPED · or REJECTED from DRAFT if it does not meet the rules

Pallet

It is opened, sealed boxes are added, and it is closed. Only a closed pallet enters a shipment.

OPEN→ CLOSED→ SHIPPED→ DELIVERED→ RECONCILED

Shipment

It is opened, closed pallets are added, and it is closed. Once marked as shipped, everything is frozen.

OPEN→ CLOSED→ SHIPPED→ DELIVERED→ RECONCILED
Roles

Who does what

Everyone operates in the same panel, but each role sees and can do different things. Your domain role (center_role) defines your scope.

Volunteer
volunteer
  • Records intakes and creates, fills and seals boxes.
  • Prints labels and QR codes.
  • Sees their own requests. Works only in their center.
Coordinator
coordinator
  • Everything a volunteer can do, plus creating and closing pallets and shipments.
  • Generates the manifest for each shipment.
  • Adds volunteers to their center and resets their passwords.
National admin
national_admin
  • Sees the national aggregate of all centers.
  • Creates and edits centers, campaigns, catalogue and any user.
  • Resolves atypical-volume reviews and reads the audit log.
  • Has no home center: when capturing, they pick which one it is for.
Superadmin
/studio
  • Operates in /studio, not the panel: this is the platform, not a center.
  • Creates national admins and resets anyone's password.
  • Reads the platform-wide audit log, email bounces and AI spend.
Rules

What the system enforces on its own

You don't have to remember them by heart: Araguaney validates them at the moment of recording and sealing.

01

Homogeneous box

A box references a single product, a single batch and a single expiry. If a mix arrives, it is split into several boxes.

02

Minimum shelf life

Medicines: at least 365 days remaining (WHO). Food and water: at least 180 days. Below that, the box is rejected automatically.

03

Complete medicines

To seal a medicine you need the denomination (INN), form, strength and batch number. If anything is missing, it cannot be sealed.

04

Controlled items blocked

A product marked as controlled is rejected at intake, without exception.
