Module · Shipments

Consolidate a shipment and generate the manifest

Steps 4 and 5, the end of the road. A shipment groups closed pallets, produces the manifest that customs needs, and once dispatched it freezes all its contents to preserve traceability.

Panel › Shipments  ·  /dashboard/shipments

What it does. Gathers closed pallets into a shipment with a destination and carrier, generates the exportable manifest (packing list), and marks the shipment as dispatched.

Why it is the decisive step. Without homogeneous boxes and a detailed manifest, the cargo gets stuck at customs. This is where all the prior order becomes a document that lets the shipment through.

The shipment status

From open to reconciled

It is filled while OPEN; it is fixed when it moves to CLOSED; and when dispatched (SHIPPED) it drags its pallets and boxes into the same status. Dispatching is not the end: DELIVERED says it arrived and RECONCILED says what arrived has been recorded. Both are covered in the Reception at destination manual.

Every transition is recorded. Dispatching is final: it freezes the entire content of the shipment, and it stays frozen afterwards. Delivering and reconciling never touch the boxes again: what arrived is stored separately, and measuring shrinkage depends on that separation.

OPEN→ CLOSED→ SHIPPED→ DELIVERED→ RECONCILED
Step by step

From the shipment to the manifest

1

Create the shipment

In Shipments, create a new one with its destination, carrier and reference. It is born open (OPEN).

2

Add closed pallets

Add to the shipment the pallets you have already closed. Only closed pallets that are not already in another shipment can be added.

A pallet won't go in? Check that it is closed and that it does not already belong to another shipment.
3

Close the shipment

When it is complete, close it. It moves to CLOSED. A shipment cannot be closed without pallets.

4

Generate the manifest

Download the manifest as a PDF (packing list) or as Excel in the IFRC format. It is assembled solely from the shipment's pallets and boxes, with no extra data entry. Generation runs in the background.

5

Dispatch

Mark the shipment as dispatched. At that moment all its pallets and boxes become shipped and the content is frozen. The cargo leaves with its manifest.

The document

What the manifest includes

It is generated automatically because each box was already registered and sealed as homogeneous. Nothing needs to be re-entered.

On dispatch

The freeze

❄

Everything is locked

When you mark the shipment as dispatched, its pallets become shipped and every box inside as well. From then on nothing is edited: the manifest reflects exactly what left, and that snapshot is kept for traceability.

Permissions

Who consolidates shipments

Creating and closing shipments and generating the manifest is the job of the coordinator in their center and of the national_admin in any center. Manifest generation is limited for security (2 per minute), so it may take a few seconds during peaks.
