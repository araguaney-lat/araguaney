Module · Requests

Ask and resolve

Requests are a channel with a message thread to ask for something or report an issue, and follow it through until it is closed.

Panel › Requests · /dashboard/requests

In short

What it does. Lets you open a request with a title and description, converse in its thread and track its status.

When it is used. When an operator needs something from the coordination or a pending item needs to be recorded.

The state of a request

The status communicates where each request stands.

From open to closed, the request goes through these states.

OPEN → IN_PROGRESS → RESOLVED → CLOSED

Step by step

1

Create the request

Open a request with a clear title and a description of what you need or are reporting.

2

Converse in the thread

Every reply is recorded in the thread and notifies the parties by email.

3

Follow the progress

Whoever manages it updates the status as the request advances.

4

Close it

Once resolved it is closed; a closed request remains as a record.

Thread rules

✕

You cannot reply to a closed request

Once closed, the thread remains as a record and does not accept new replies.

Cannot reply to a closed request
✕

Invalid status

The status can only take one of the values defined in the state machine.

Status must be one of ('OPEN', 'IN_PROGRESS', 'RESOLVED', 'CLOSED')

Permissions

Any operator creates requests and takes part in the thread of their own. Changing the status and managing them belongs to the national_admin (and the coordinator within their scope). Email notifies the parties when there are updates.
