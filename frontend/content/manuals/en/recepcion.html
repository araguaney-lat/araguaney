Module · Intake

Record a donation

Intake is step 1 and the most used: this is where every donation enters Araguaney, item by item. Everything else —boxes, pallets, shipments, manifests— is born from what is captured here.

Panel › Intake › New intake  ·  /dashboard/intake/new

What it does. It turns a donation that has just arrived into one or more registered homogeneous boxes, each with its product, quantity, batch and expiry. A single intake can generate several boxes.

When it's used. Every time something arrives at the center: one person receives, another records. Recording well here is what later makes it possible to know what you have and prepare shipments that clear customs.

Step by step

How to record an intake

One box at a time. If the same donation brings two products, or the same product in two batches, that's two boxes.

1

Open a new intake

Go to Intake in the menu and click New intake. The form opens with the first box ready to fill.

2

Identify the product

Scan the barcode or search by name. If the code exists, Araguaney autocompletes the product. If the product is not in the catalog, it is created as a new product type.

If the product is marked as controlled, the box is blocked right here: it is not accepted.
3

Capture the box data

Quantity and unit, batch number, expiry date and, if you have it, the weight. This data defines the box and feeds the manifest later on.

4

Did more arrive? Add another box

If the donation brings another product, or another batch or expiry of the same one, click Add box and repeat. Remember: never mix more than one product, batch or expiry in a single box.

5

Note the source (optional)

You can enter a free-text donor —short text, no personal data— and internal notes. Araguaney does not store personal information about donors or beneficiaries.

6

Save

On save, each box is created and validated instantly. Depending on whether it meets the rules, it ends up as a draft ready to seal, or rejected with the reason.

On save

What happens to each box

Each box is evaluated separately. Within the same intake, some may end up as drafts and others rejected.

DRAFT

Meets the rules. It stays as a draft, ready to be sealed in the Boxes module. On sealing it receives its QR code and label.

REJECTED

Fails one of the rules. It is recorded anyway —for the record— but marked as rejected, with the exact reason. It does not advance to a pallet or shipment.

Diagnosis

Why a box is rejected

These are the exact reasons Araguaney shows. The message tells you what to correct.

✕

Controlled product

The product type is marked as controlled. It is not accepted at collection centers, without exception.

Controlled product: not accepted at collection centers.
✕

Missing expiry

The category demands shelf life but the box has no expiry date.

Expiry date required for category MEDICINE.
✕

Insufficient shelf life

Fewer days remain than the category demands (365 medicines, 180 food and water).

Insufficient shelf life: 120 days remaining, minimum required 365 days.
✕

Incomplete medicine

The product type is missing required fields (INN, form or strength), or the box is missing the batch.

Batch number required for medicines.
Permissions

Who can record

Intakes are recorded by the volunteer and the coordinator in their own center, and by the national_admin in any center. The intake is assigned to a campaign; every new user is placed in the Donaciones Generales campaign by default, and can only record in campaigns they are a member of.
