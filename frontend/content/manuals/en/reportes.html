Module · Reports

How much has been gathered

Reports summarize, by campaign, how much inventory has been gathered — broken down by category and with the consolidated weight.

Panel › Reports · /dashboard/reports

What it does. Gives a per-campaign summary of the stock gathered.

What for. To size up the progress of a cause and communicate it.

What the report shows

By category

How much there is of medicine, food, water, etc. within the campaign.

Consolidated weight

The total weight gathered for that campaign.

How to use it

1

Choose the campaign

Select the campaign you want to review, from the ones available to you.

2

Read the summary

Review the breakdown by category and the total weight.

Permissions

The coordinator sees the reports for their center's campaigns; the national_admin sees the reports for all of them.
