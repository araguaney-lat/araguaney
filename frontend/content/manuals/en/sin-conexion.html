Capturing in a basement

Many collection centres operate in basements and warehouses with no coverage. Araguaney stores the capture on the phone and sends it by itself once the signal returns.

Panel › Intake › Pending · /dashboard/intake/pending

What it does. Registers donations with no coverage, with the label printed on the spot, and delivers them to the server as soon as there is signal.

How it works. The capture is stored on the device with an identifier of its own. That identifier never changes, so retrying the delivery can never duplicate inventory even if the answer was lost halfway.

What it does not do. It does not seal boxes, build pallets or close shipments offline: those depend on the state of boxes that may be changing on another device.

Before going down

With signal is the only moment you can prepare. It takes thirty seconds.

1

Open the app with coverage

On opening, it downloads the product catalogue for your campaigns and sets aside a block of box codes.

2

Check there is something to work with

In Intake › Pending you will see how many products are downloaded and how many codes are set aside. If codes run low, they are topped up automatically whenever you open the app with signal.

3

Go down with the app open

Do not close it. If you close it and the basement has no signal, the app will not be able to load again.

What you can do without signal

Everything that depends only on what is in front of you.

Register an intake

Campaign, donor, notes and as many boxes as needed, exactly as with a connection.

Pick products from the catalogue

The search uses the catalogue you downloaded, with the same campaign visibility you would see online.

Scan known barcodes

If the code was already associated with a product, it resolves on the device. If not, you pick the product by hand.

Print the label

The QR is drawn on the phone with the code set aside in advance, and points to the same public page it will point to afterwards.

What you cannot do without signal

This is not a technical gap waiting to be fixed: these operations depend on the state of other boxes.

Seal boxes, build pallets, close shipments

A box may have entered a pallet from another device. Deciding blind would produce two truths about the same box.

Look up a new barcode

The query to the global product database needs the internet. Pick the product by hand and the catalogue learns that association on sync.

National dashboard, reports, transfers

These are reads from the server. Whatever they showed offline would be out of date with no way to know by how much.

Edit a capture already saved

It is captured or it is not. If something went wrong, the capture stays visible in Pending so you can discard it and do it again.

The step that matters most

On leaving the basement, open the app and wait for the counter to reach zero.

The pending-captures counter lives in the panel frame and is visible from any screen. Until it reads zero, those captures exist only on that phone.

If you close the app with captures pending, the browser warns you. Do not ignore it: nobody else can send them for you, from another device or another account.

If something does not arrive

Nothing is discarded on its own. Everything ends up in front of a person.

1

The server rejected the capture

Because of a rule the device could not check: a shelf life that is too short, a controlled product, a campaign that already closed. It appears in Pending with the exact reason. Retrying would give the same answer, so it waits for someone to correct or discard it.

2

It failed several times in a row

After a few attempts it stops retrying by itself and goes to review. Retrying forever would burn battery and data while hiding the problem behind a counter that never drops.

3

Your session expired while it waited

The queue is left untouched: sign in again and it sends itself. Captures are not lost because a session expired.

Limits worth knowing

One device, one person

The queue only sends the captures of whoever made them. If you lend the phone, the other person's captures wait until that person signs in.

Reloading in the basement

The app loads from the copy the browser stored. A screen you never visited with signal may not open.

If codes run out

The capture is saved anyway — losing it would be far worse — but with no printable label. Its labels come from the server PDF once the signal returns.

Private browsing

Some browsers refuse to store anything on the device. The app says so rather than pretending it saved.

Permissions

Offline capture is for centre staff (volunteer, coordinator). A national_admin has no home centre and therefore no code block to draw labels from: they register on behalf of a centre with a connection.
