The product catalog

The catalog is the product types (SKUs) you can register in the app. Each type defines its category and its attributes, and it is what makes two centers speak the same language.

Panel › Catalog · /dashboard/catalog

What it is

What the catalog is for

It is the foundation everything else rests on: without a product type you cannot register a donation or seal a box.

What it does. It maintains the product types that can be registered across the whole operation. When you enter a donation, you always choose a product type from the catalog.

Why it matters. A well-defined ProductType is what makes it possible to validate donations (expiry, controlled medicines) and aggregate stock across centers. If everyone names and classifies things the same way, the national dashboard can total them up with a single GROUP BY.

Structure

Anatomy of a product type

These are the fields that define each product type. Medicines use additional attributes.

Name
The product's visible name (display_name). It is what you see when searching and when choosing a type at intake.
Category
One of 8: Medicines, Medical supplies, Food, Water, Hygiene, Tools, Rescue gear and Other. It determines which rules apply to the product.
Medicine attributes
For medicines: inn_name (INN), form (form) and strength (strength). They distinguish one medicine from another.
Controlled
is_controlled: if enabled, the product is blocked at intake and cannot be received.
Barcode
gtin, optional. It is validated as EAN-8, UPC-A or EAN-13.
Minimum shelf life
min_shelf_life_days, optional. If you don't set it, the category's own minimum applies.
Default unit
The unit proposed when registering quantities (default_unit).
Key rule

An attribute changes the SKU

Attributes are not cosmetic: they define the product's identity.

Ibuprofen 500 mg and Ibuprofen 900 mg are different product types, because their strength (strength) differs.

That is intentional: it lets you count each one separately and avoids mixing products that are not the same. If the attribute changes, the SKU changes.

Reuse

Promote to global

So a type created at one center serves the whole network.

A product type created within a center can be promoted to global so that all centers reuse it. This prevents each center from creating its own version of the same product.

Only the national_admin promotes a product type to global.

Permissions

Who can do what

The whole operational team consults the catalog; editing it is the national admin's job.

Searching and viewing the catalog is done by all operational roles: volunteer, coordinator and national_admin.

Creating and editing product types and promoting to global is the job of the national_admin.
