Module · Users

Create and manage accounts

This is where operators are set up, assigned a role and center, and have their password reset when needed.

Panel › Users · /dashboard/admin/users

What it does. Creates users, assigns them a role and center, and manages their access to the application.

Base rule. Who can create whom depends on your own role.

Who can create whom

The role you operate with determines which roles you can assign and at which center.

Who creates Roles they assign Center
superadmin national_admin (and any) any center or none
national_admin volunteer, coordinator any center
coordinator volunteer only their own center only

Invitation flow

From account creation to the user's first sign-in.

1

The admin creates the user

The system generates a temporary password and sends an invitation email.

2

The user signs in with that password

The system requires them to change their password (must_change_password).

3

The user manages their access

From their profile they can change their password whenever they want.

Every new user is automatically assigned to the “Donaciones Generales” campaign.

Reset password

The button is per row in the user list; it generates a new temporary password and resends the email. The scope depends on your role.

superadmin
Can reset the password of any user.
national_admin
Can reset coordinators and volunteers. Not other national_admins.
coordinator
Only volunteers at their own center.

Permissions

Domain user management is handled by the national_admin; the coordinator only adds and resets volunteers at their own center. national_admin accounts are created and managed by the superadmin from the Studio.
