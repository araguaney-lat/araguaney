Module · Audit

The record of everything that happens

The audit is the read-only log of sensitive actions: who did what, to what, and when.

Panel › Audit · /dashboard/admin/audit

What it is for

What it does. Keeps a record of the system's important actions for accountability. Every sensitive action is logged with its author, its object, and its moment.

Why it matters. In an operation with many hands, being able to answer "who did this" avoids pointing fingers blindly. The record replaces suspicion with a fact you can look up.

What each entry records

Every log entry gathers the same five data points, so any action can be identified without ambiguity.

Action
What was done. For example, closing or dispatching a shipment.
Affected entity
What the action fell on: the box, the pallet, the shipment, the user…
User
Who carried it out.
Date and time
The exact moment it happened.
Context
Extra data about the change, to understand what was modified and with which values.

Two layers of trail

The trail is kept at two complementary levels: the history of each piece and the view of the whole system.

Events per piece

Each box, pallet, and shipment keeps its own history of status changes, visible on its record. There you see the lifeline of that specific piece.

Cross-cutting audit

This module gathers the actions of the whole system in one place. It is for looking at the complete operation, not a single isolated piece.

Read only

The audit cannot be edited or deleted from the app. It is a record to consult, not to modify. That immutability is exactly what gives it value as evidence.

Permissions

The domain audit is consulted by the national_admin. The superadmin additionally sees the platform's general audit from the Studio. volunteer and coordinator do not access the audit.
