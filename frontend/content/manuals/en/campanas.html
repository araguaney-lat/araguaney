Module · Campaigns

Organize donations by cause

A campaign groups the donations of an event or a cause —an earthquake, a drive— and gives it its own public "what's needed" page.

Panel › Campaigns · /dashboard/campaigns

What it does. It lets you create and manage campaigns: each one is a named container under which donations are registered.

Why it matters. It separates and communicates what is being gathered for each cause without mixing inventories. This way you can report the progress of each event separately and direct people to the right cause.

Structure

What defines a campaign

Three pieces of data describe each campaign. The public link is built automatically; you only choose the name and the status.

Name
The title of the cause or the event. It is what you see in the panel and what the public link is derived from.
Public link
The campaign's slug. It is generated automatically from the name. If it clashes with another campaign's, Araguaney adds a suffix so it stays unique.
Status
The campaign can be active or inactive. It controls whether it is still in operation or closed.
Default campaign

Donaciones Generales

Donaciones Generales is the default campaign. It always exists and works as a safety net.

Every new user is assigned to it automatically, and any intake that does not specify a particular campaign falls there. Nothing is lost: if you didn't choose a campaign, the donation is registered under Donaciones Generales.

Public page

Each campaign has its public page

The per-event traffic magnet: a link you can share so people see what is needed.

At /eventos/{slug} anyone can see, without login, the inventory available for that campaign.

The page updates every 5 minutes, so it reflects what is available at the moment. Share that link over social media or messaging to attract donations to the right cause.

Who can register

Members

Only the members of a campaign can register donations in it. Being a member is what enables a person to add intakes to that cause.

The coordinator manages the campaign members of their own center. The national_admin manages campaigns and their members at the national level.

Permissions

Who can do what

Creating and editing campaigns is the job of the national_admin. The coordinator manages the campaign members of their center, but does not create or edit campaigns.
