Module · Pallets

Build a pallet

Step 3. A pallet groups already-sealed boxes to move them together. Unlike a box, a pallet can be mixed: it can carry different products. It has its own QR code and, once closed, it enters a shipment.

Panel › Pallets  ·  /dashboard/pallets

What it does. It lets you create pallets, put sealed boxes in them and close them. It is the bridge between loose boxes and a consolidated shipment.

The golden rule. Only sealed boxes from your center, that are not already in another pallet, can enter. While the pallet is open you can add and remove; once closed, it is fixed.

The pallet status

Open, closed, shipped

It is filled while OPEN; it is frozen when it moves to CLOSED; and it moves to SHIPPED automatically when its shipment is dispatched.

Every change is recorded with who and when. There is no going back: a closed pallet is not reopened.

OPEN→ CLOSED→ SHIPPED
Step by step

How to build and close a pallet

1

Create a pallet

In Pallets, create a new one. It is born open (OPEN) and with its own QR code. You can add a note to it.

2

Add sealed boxes

Scan each box's QR code or type its code to add it to the pallet. It only accepts sealed boxes from your center that are not already in another pallet.

A box won't go in? Almost always it's because it is still a draft: seal it first.
3

Review and adjust

While the pallet is still open you can remove boxes you added by mistake. Take the chance to confirm the contents are what you want to ship.

4

Close the pallet

When it's complete, close it. It moves to CLOSED and no longer accepts or releases boxes. An empty pallet cannot be closed.

5

Label it and add it to the shipment

Stick on its QR label. A closed pallet can now be added to a shipment in the next module.

Requirements

Which box can enter

Box status
Must be sealed. A box that is a draft, rejected or already shipped does not enter.
Same center
The box must belong to your center. Boxes from another center are not mixed in.
No double assignment
A box can only be in one pallet at a time.
Open pallet
Only an OPEN pallet accepts or releases boxes. Once closed it is fixed.
Diagnosis

Why something won't work

The exact notices from Araguaney. The text tells you what to correct.

✕

The box is not sealed

Only sealed boxes enter. Seal the box in the Boxes module and try again.

Only SEALED boxes can be added to a pallet
✕

The pallet is already closed

A closed pallet does not accept more boxes. If you need changes, create another pallet.

Only OPEN pallets accept boxes
✕

The box is already in another pallet

Each box goes in a single pallet. Remove it from the other one if it really must change.

Box … is already assigned to a pallet
✕

Empty pallet

A pallet cannot be closed without boxes. Add at least one.

Cannot close an empty pallet
Permissions

Who builds pallets

Creating and closing pallets is the job of the coordinator in their center and the national_admin in any center. The volunteer prepares and seals the boxes, but does not close pallets or shipments.
