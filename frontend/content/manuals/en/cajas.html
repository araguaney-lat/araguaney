Module · Boxes

Seal a box

Step 2. A box is born as a draft during intake; sealing it is what makes it official: it receives its QR code and its label, and becomes ready to enter a pallet. Only a sealed box can travel.

Panel › Boxes  ·  /dashboard/boxes

What it does. It gathers all the center's boxes and lets you seal them and print their labels. You can filter by status (draft, sealed, rejected, shipped) to see exactly what you need.

Why it matters. Sealing freezes the box's contents and gives it a traceable identity. From then on it is no longer edited: what was sealed is what travels.

The box status

From draft to sealed

Sealing moves the box from DRAFT to SEALED. That step is the gateway to everything that follows.

Every change is recorded with who and when. Sealing is irreversible: if something is wrong, the box is handled separately, it is not "un-sealed".

DRAFT→ SEALED→ SHIPPED · or REJECTED from intake, if it did not meet the rules
Step by step

How to seal

1

Open Boxes and filter by draft

Go to Boxes in the menu. Filter by Draft to see only the ones still to be sealed.

2

Review the box

Confirm that product, quantity, batch and expiry are correct. Remember: a box is a single product, a single batch, a single expiry.

3

Click Seal

Araguaney validates the box instantly. If it is a medicine, it checks that its data is complete (see requirements below). If everything is fine, the box moves to Sealed.

Before sealing you can preview the QR code of the label.
4

Print the labels

Click Download labels to generate a letter-size PDF with the labels (10 per sheet) of the filtered boxes. Generation runs in the background; as soon as it's ready, you download the PDF.

5

Label it and on to the pallet

Stick the label with its QR code on the physical box. Once sealed, the box becomes available to group into a pallet.

Requirements

What a box needs to be sealed

Most categories require nothing extra at sealing — the expiry was already validated at intake. Medicines are the exception.

Any box
Must be in draft status (DRAFT). A box that is already sealed, shipped or rejected cannot be sealed again.
Medicines
In addition, the product type must have a denomination (INN), form and strength; and the box must have a batch and expiry.
Other categories
Do not require additional fields to seal (supplies, food, water, hygiene, tools, rescue gear).
Diagnosis

Why a box won't seal

These are the exact notices from Araguaney. Each one tells you what to correct in order to seal.

✕

Not in draft

Only draft boxes can be sealed. One already sealed or shipped does not apply.

Box is in status 'SEALED'; only DRAFT boxes can be sealed
✕

Missing medicine data

The product type is missing INN, form or strength. Complete them in the Catalog and seal again.

Cannot seal: product type missing required fields: inn_name, form, strength
✕

Missing batch

A medicine cannot be sealed without a batch number.

Batch is required to seal a medicine box
✕

Missing expiry

A medicine cannot be sealed without an expiry date.

Expiry date is required to seal a medicine box
After sealing

What the box gains

QR code + label

Every sealed box has its own unique QR code. When scanned, anyone can see its minimal public record.

Ready for a pallet

Only sealed boxes can be grouped into a pallet. It's the requirement of the next step.

Frozen contents

No longer edited. What was sealed is what will appear on the shipment manifest.

Permissions

Who can seal

Boxes are sealed and labels printed by the volunteer and the coordinator in their own center, and by the national_admin in any center. Label generation into PDF is limited for security (rate limit), so during peaks it can take a few seconds.
