What is yours to do

The other manuals each explain one module. This one explains one role: what you do on a normal day, in what order, and what you cannot do even when you can see the button.

Panel › Help · /dashboard/ayuda

Two different roles, not one. Your center_role (volunteer, coordinator, national admin) decides what you can do inside the panel. Superadmin is not a centre role: it operates in /studio, which is the platform.

If something does not show up, it is almost always one of two things: your role does not reach it, or you are not enrolled in that campaign. The second is by far the more common one, and your coordination fixes it in a minute.

volunteer

Volunteer

You are at the counter. Your job is that what comes in is recorded properly, and that every box leaves labelled.

1

Open the app when your shift starts

With signal. It downloads the catalogue and sets aside box codes, which is what lets you keep working if the centre is in a basement. See Offline.

2

Receive the donation

If the person brings a QR starting with DN-, scan it and do the double check. If not, go straight to Intake → New.

3

Capture: one line per box

The box is born here, not before. Product, quantity, unit, batch, expiry and weight. On save, every box is created at once with its own code.

4

Seal and print

Sealing is the promise that what the label says is what is inside. A sealed box is no longer editable.

What you cannot do, and who to ask. You do not build pallets or shipments: that is your coordination's. You do not create catalogue products: that is the national admin's, and it is worth asking before the doors open, because a capture stops dead if the product is missing.

coordinator

Coordinator

Everything the volunteer does, plus what turns loose boxes into a shipment with documents.

Build pallets

Only sealed boxes go in. On closing you capture the scale weight and the height. If the scale is broken, it closes anyway.

Close and dispatch shipments

Only closed pallets go in. Dispatching is the point of no return: it freezes everything, and it stays frozen afterwards.

Generate the documents

Manifest in PDF and Excel, and the goods declaration. They are built in the background; the screen tells you when they are ready.

Record the journey and what arrived

Milestones along the way, delivery, box-by-box reception and incidents. See Reception at destination.

Look after your team

You create volunteers for your centre and reset their passwords. Not for another centre.

Resolve reviews

Captures escalated for atypical volume. You cannot resolve one you opened yourself: that goes up to your national admin.

What you cannot do. Create centres, campaigns or catalogue products. See other centres. You also cannot create another coordinator: that is the national admin's.

national_admin

National admin

You prepare the ground before the first donation arrives, and you see the whole once it is running.

1

Create the centres

With legal name, tax identification and address: these are printed as-is on the transport documents later. Nobody else can capture them.

2

Seed the catalogue

This is the step that jams an operation most if left for later. A volunteer looks products up; they do not invent them.

3

Open the campaign with its people in it

Picking participating centres enrols all their active users. Someone not enrolled cannot capture for that campaign or see its report.

4

Watch the whole

The national aggregate, volume reviews, requests and the audit log.

You have no home centre. That is why capturing an intake asks you which centre it is for, and why offline capture is not for you: there is no block of box codes that belongs to you.

superadmin · /studio

Platform

Does not operate a centre. Keeps the platform working for everyone who does.

Accounts

Creates national admins, blocks and unblocks anyone, resets passwords.

Platform-wide audit

The log for the whole platform, not for one centre.

Email

Bounces and complaints, with resend. An invitation email that never arrives leaves someone locked out and nobody finds out.

AI spend

This month's consumption per capability and the state of each switch. The panel only reads: switching on or off is done in the server configuration.

Questions that always come up

No campaign shows up for me

You are not enrolled in any. Your coordination or the national admin adds you in a minute.

My report comes out empty

Almost always the date range: it defaults to the last 30 days, and it counts by capture date.

I cannot find the product

It is missing from the catalogue. Only the national admin creates products.

I cannot seal a box

If it is a medicine, it is missing a batch, an expiry, or the catalogue product has no INN, form and strength.
