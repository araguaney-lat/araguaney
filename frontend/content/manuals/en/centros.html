Module · Centers

The collection centers

Each collection center is its own space (a tenant): its inventory is isolated from the others', and the national dashboard totals them all.

Panel › Centers · /dashboard/centers

What this module is

What it does. It lets you register and manage the coordination's centers.

Why it matters. The center is the base unit: every user, box, pallet and shipment belongs to a center.

What defines a center

Name
The name the collection center is identified by within the coordination.
Country
The country where the center operates (country_code).
Status
Indicates whether the center is active or inactive.

Data isolation

This model is what makes national aggregation cheap.

Each center sees its own

A center's operators only see and touch their own inventory. Nothing they register is mixed with that of other centers.

The national level totals them

The national_admin, with no assigned center (center_id NULL), sees the aggregate of all centers at once.

Permissions

Creating and editing centers is the national_admin's. The volunteer and coordinator operate within their center, but do not create centers.
