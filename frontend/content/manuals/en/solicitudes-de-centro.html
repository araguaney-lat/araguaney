Module · Center applications

Review and approve new centers

When a center registers itself from the website, its application lands here for your review. Approving it creates the center and makes its contact a coordinator.

Panel › Administration › Center applications · /dashboard/admin/center-applications

What it does. It's the review queue for center self-registration. An application is not a center yet: it only becomes one when you approve it.

Why it exists. Registration is open, but human review keeps out fake centers. The application is the quarantine.

How an application reaches your queue

The applicant clears two filters before you see it.

1

They register on the web

They fill the public form /register-center (center + contact + trust signals), with an anti-bot Turnstile check.

2

They confirm their email

They get an email and click to verify. Only then does the application move to PENDING_REVIEW and appear in your queue.

If they never confirm, the application stays in PENDING_EMAIL and never reaches you.
3

We notify you

You receive an email that an application is ready to review. You also see a badge in the menu.

What you see and what to review

Each application carries trust signals to help you decide.

Field Purpose
Center · Country · State · Address Locate and validate the center.
Contact · Email · Phone The operational contact who will become the coordinator.
Backing organization Trust signal: who vouches for the center.
Social / website Public link to verify the center is real.
Message Free-text context the applicant left.

Approve or reject

Two paths, each with its effect and its automatic email.

Approve
The Center is created (only now does it enter the national aggregate), the contact becomes the center's coordinator and gets an invitation email with a temporary password. They're assigned to the General Donations campaign.
Reject
You write a reason. The applicant gets an email with that reason and can register again fixing whatever is needed.

Which applications you see

Scope depends on your role.

national_admin
Only applications from your country.
superadmin
All of them, from Studio › Center applications. Also gets the notification when a country has no national_admin yet.

If the invitation doesn't arrive

On approval the invitation with the temporary password is sent. If the email bounces (mistyped address), the superadmin sees it in Studio › Emails and can resend it (generates a new password and resends).
